Who operates Nariz Flow?

Nariz Flow is provided by Studio Nariz (스튜디오 나리즈), a business in Seoul, South Korea. For app support, privacy questions or a request concerning information you sent to us, email contact@studionariz.com. This policy covers Nariz Flow for macOS and separates its local app data from optional support correspondence.

What the app records on your Mac

Recording starts only after you choose a shortcut and agree to start recording. It captures new supported text, rich-text/HTML formatting, URLs and images copied while recording is active. It also stores information needed to manage history, including copy times, estimated source application, usage counts, pins, titles and work groups. File copies are not supported. Copies made before recording begins or resumes are not imported retroactively. Source app labels are estimates, not proof of who created clipboard content.

Local processing and transfers

Clipboard history is stored on your Mac. The app has no in-app account, app-operated server, cloud sync, advertising SDK or analytics SDK, and does not automatically send clipboard content, searches or diagnostics to Studio Nariz or an AI service. Editing an image and cleaning text happen locally. When you paste, the selected content is supplied to the destination app you choose; that app’s own data practices apply. Optional links open in your web browser and are handled separately by the destination website. App Store purchases, installation and optional system diagnostics are handled by Apple under its own terms and privacy settings.

Mac Assistant and Apple Intelligence

Eight built-in Mac guides and their favourite identifiers work without a cloud service. Where the system supports and enables Apple Intelligence, the app may use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework to classify only the question you type into an allowed guide topic. Clipboard content, files and screen contents are not supplied to that model. Questions are not saved by the app. Built-in guides remain available when Apple Intelligence is unavailable. This version does not provide automatic translation, OCR or a general-purpose chatbot.

Encryption and its limits

Clipboard content, stored image data, user-written titles and group names are encrypted locally with CryptoKit (AES-GCM). The key is stored in macOS Keychain. Some operational database fields, such as record IDs, timestamps, item sizes, pin order and group associations, are not encrypted. Content used for search, previews and thumbnails is available in memory while the app runs. These protections do not erase the system clipboard, secure the destination app, or guarantee that content is inaccessible to someone who controls your unlocked Mac.

Retention, pausing and deletion

Defaults are 30 days for ordinary records, a maximum of 5,000 records and a total storage limit of 512 MiB. Pinned records do not expire automatically but still count toward count and size limits. You can change retention options, pause capture, exclude apps and delete records or reset app history. A recent batch deletion can be undone for 10 seconds. App deletion actions do not clear the system clipboard, remove OS backups or guarantee secure erasure from SSD/APFS snapshots. A Keychain access failure or damaged store is not silently reset or replaced with plaintext storage.

Permissions and sensitive content

Clipboard access is used to read new copies, including a system consent prompt where macOS requires one. Accessibility/event-posting permission is used only for the explicit selected-item paste flow. The app does not monitor typed keys, use Input Monitoring or request Full Disk Access. Excluded apps and sensitive clipboard markers reduce capture, but the app cannot detect every password, secret or sensitive image. You can pause recording before copying material you do not want saved. A separately saved diagnostic file requires a destination that you choose.

Diagnostics and support emails

Diagnostic information is exported only when you choose to save a file. It omits clipboard content, search queries, custom group names and file paths, and is not sent automatically. If you choose to email support, Studio Nariz receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide, for answering and managing the request. Mail delivery and forwarding use external email providers, separately from the app; those services may process messages using their own infrastructure and policies. Do not include passwords or sensitive clipboard history. Support correspondence is kept while a request is active and for up to 90 days after resolution, then deleted from the active support mailbox. Material that must be retained by applicable law is kept separately for the required period and used only for that purpose. To request access, correction or deletion of a support message, contact the address above; we may need enough information to identify the relevant correspondence. You do not need to send us your local clipboard history to make a request.

Policy changes and related services

This page will show its update date when data practices change. The separate support page explains recording, shortcuts and permission setup. The Nariz Labs website is served through GitHub Pages; ordinary website requests may be processed by the hosting service under its own privacy practices. This app policy is not a promise about another website, destination app, email provider or Apple service.
