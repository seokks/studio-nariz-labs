Clear about your data

Your clipboard content
stays on your Mac.

Text, images and names you add are encrypted locally. The app does not upload clipboard history, require an in-app account or include advertising and analytics SDKs.

Operational metadata such as timestamps and item sizes is not encrypted. Sensitive-item filters reduce capture but cannot identify every secret. You control recording, exclusions and retention.

Before the first paste

A clear start, and a way to get help.

How do I open my clipboard history?

Choose a shortcut at first launch; the default is ⇧⌘V. The clipboard icon in the menu bar also opens history. Dock visibility is optional in Settings.

Why is a new copy missing?

Check that recording is on and the app or item is not excluded. Select All history, then copy again. Copies made before recording starts or resumes are not imported retroactively.

Why does double-click not paste?

Put the cursor in the destination field before opening history. Check Setup & permissions and allow the exact installed app in macOS Accessibility settings. Recording permission and direct-paste permission are separate.

Is translation or cloud sync included?

Automatic translation, OCR and cloud sync are not included in this version. Mac Assistant offers eight built-in guides; it is not a general-purpose question-answering service.
